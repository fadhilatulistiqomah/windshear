Atmospheric Motion Vector (AMV) approximation

Select the earlier and later Himawari NetCDF files separately. Their observation times must be 10 minutes apart. The app automatically processes Band 3 (visible), Band 7 (3.9 μm infrared), and Band 13 (10.4 μm infrared).

Color raster shows interpolated apparent feature speed; arrows point in tracked motion direction and are enlarged for visibility. Paper-inspired forward/backward feature tracking only—not a validated wind or operational product. Cloud-height assignment is not implemented.
